# Tank Background 30 Prompt Set

Goal: create 30 portrait tank background assets that cover common player favorite colors while still feeling like varied aquarium biomes. These backgrounds should sit behind fish, coins, decorations, and separate seabed assets, so they must stay readable, spacious, and low-contrast.

Generation format:

- Source/runtime target size: `860x1505` portrait image.
- Runtime format later: WebP under `public/assets/backgrounds/generated-bg/`.
- No transparency or chroma key needed.
- Generate one full background per image, not a sheet.
- Keep the bottom area compatible with separate seabed sprites. Do not bake in sand, rocks, or a floor.

## Shared Style Lock

Paste this exact style lock into every individual generation prompt:

> Style lock: create a single full portrait aquarium tank background for a mobile casual aquarium game, 860x1505. Soft toy-like painterly rendering, clean readable shapes, gently immersive underwater lighting, low-to-medium contrast, and enough open water in the center for fish gameplay. Use a subtle water surface glow near the top, soft depth gradient, gentle side silhouettes or distant biome hints, and faint particulate/bubble details only. Do not include fish, animals, coins, food, UI, text, frame, foreground decorations, seabed, sand floor, hard horizon line, hard horizontal seam, black bars, or busy center detail. The lower 20% must remain visually calm so a separate seabed sprite can sit on top. Output one image only.

## Negative Prompt

Use this negative prompt for all tank backgrounds:

> no fish, no animals, no characters, no UI, no icons, no text, no watermark, no frame, no sand floor, no seabed, no foreground rocks, no foreground coral, no decorations, no treasure chest, no hard horizontal line, no obvious tile seam, no black bars, no photorealistic stock image, no dark muddy center, no high-detail clutter in the center, no harsh vignette, no extreme blur, no overexposed white center.
